# SmartFlow: Questions for the client / Mga tanong para sa kliyente

Prepared 19 October 2026. Please answer what you can; "not sure yet" is a fine answer. /
Sagutin ang kaya; ayos lang ang "hindi pa sigurado."

## A. Hosting and access / Hosting at pag-access

1. **Domain.** What web address do you want (for example `smartflow.urbiztondo.gov.ph`)? Who controls its DNS? /
   Anong web address ang gusto ninyo? Sino ang may hawak ng DNS?
2. **Where it runs.** Municipal office server or PC, or a paid cloud server? Who pays and who maintains it? /
   Sa server o PC ng munisipyo, o sa cloud? Sino ang magbabayad at magmamantini?
3. **Who can reach it.** Inside the municipal network only, or from the internet (other buildings, phones on mobile data)? /
   Sa loob lang ng network ng munisipyo, o pati sa internet?
   - *Why we ask:* the web camera scan only works on a secure address (HTTPS). Offices other than the server PC need it.
4. **Mobile app.** Install the APK directly on office phones, or publish to the Play Store? How many phones, and which models? /
   I-install ang APK sa mga phone ng opisina, o ilalagay sa Play Store? Ilang phone at anong modelo?

## B. Email / Email

5. **Official sender address.** Is there an official municipal email (not a personal Gmail) the system can send password-reset codes from? /
   May opisyal bang email ng munisipyo (hindi personal na Gmail) para sa password reset?
6. **Real staff emails.** Who will give us each user's real email address? Right now most are placeholders
   (`username@urbiztondo.gov.ph`), so reset codes may not reach them. /
   Sino ang magbibigay ng totoong email ng bawat user? Karamihan ngayon ay placeholder.

## C. People and volume / Mga tao at dami

7. **Users and offices at launch.** How many offices and how many staff, heads and administrators? /
   Ilang opisina at ilang staff, head at admin sa simula?
8. **Who is the system administrator** (accounts, sign-up approvals, thresholds, backups)? And a backup person? /
   Sino ang system administrator? May kapalit ba?

## D. Printing / Pag-print

9. **Label printing.** What printer and paper will offices use for the folder QR label: plain A4 (cut out by hand) or a sticker/label roll? If a roll, what size? /
   Anong printer at papel ang gagamitin sa QR label: A4 na puputulin, o sticker roll? Anong laki?
   - *Why we ask:* the label has no fixed paper size, so on a normal printer it prints small on a full sheet.
10. **Contents on the label.** Is it fine if a very long folder title or many contents lines make the label longer, or should it be cut to a fixed size? /
    Ayos lang bang humaba ang label kapag mahaba ang pamagat o marami ang laman, o dapat fixed ang laki?

## E. Rules and policy / Mga patakaran

11. **Required attachments (Office checklists).** Will offices publish a list of required attachments per document type (for example what a Disbursement Voucher must include)? This is on hold in the mobile app until you decide. /
    Maglalagay ba ang mga opisina ng listahan ng kinakailangang kalakip bawat uri ng dokumento? Naka-hold ito sa mobile hangga't wala ang desisyon ninyo.
12. **Seeing other offices' folders.** Today, looking up a folder by its ID can show a folder that belongs to another office. Should staff only see their own office's folders, plus folders linked to their requests? /
    Sa ngayon, kapag hinanap ang folder gamit ang ID, maaaring makita ang folder ng ibang opisina. Dapat bang sariling opisina lang, kasama ang mga folder na nakakabit sa kanilang request?
13. **Disbursement Voucher route.** The route (Engineering, Budget, Accounting, Treasury, Mayor, Treasury) is guidance, not enforced step by step. Is that what you want, or should skipping a step be blocked? /
    Ang ruta ng DV ay gabay lang at hindi sapilitan ang bawat hakbang. Iyon ba ang gusto, o dapat harangin ang paglaktaw?

## F. Data and safety / Data at kaligtasan

14. **Backups.** Who takes backups, how often, and where are copies kept? We also need to practice restoring one before launch. /
    Sino ang magba-backup, gaano kadalas, at saan itatago? Kailangan din nating subukan ang pagbawi bago ang launch.
15. **Old test records.** Before real use we will clear the test activity log (QR monitor). Is that acceptable, or must any test history be kept? /
    Lilinisin natin ang test activity log (QR monitor) bago ang tunay na paggamit. Puwede ba, o may kailangang itago?
