# SmartFlow — Client Discovery Questionnaire

**Municipality of Urbiztondo · Office of the Municipal Accountant**  
**Purpose:** To validate document workflows, roles, and system rules before finalizing the SmartFlow pilot (Engineering, Human Resources, Budget, and Accounting), and to document how municipal practice aligns with Commission on Audit (COA) expectations for supporting documents, internal control, and audit preparation.

- **Respondent:** Municipal Accountant / Head of Accounting  
- **Prepared by:** SmartFlow Capstone Team  
- **Estimated Time:** 45–60 minutes  
- **Note:** SmartFlow is a document movement and custody tracking tool. It does not replace COA rules on approvals, signatures, or financial validation.
