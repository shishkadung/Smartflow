# Figure 1.2 - SmartFlow System Use Case Diagram

This use case diagram reflects the implemented SmartFlow system as of 29 September 2026.

## Actors

- **Staff / Clerk** performs assigned-office document registration, QR scanning, custody movement, tracking, and document-request work.
- **Department Head** inherits the Staff / Clerk capabilities and additionally monitors the assigned office queue, analytics, and delays.
- **Municipal Accountant / Admin** performs municipality-wide administration, monitoring, audit review, and COA flow reporting. The admin may also perform shared document operations for the account's assigned office.

Treasury, Mayor, Budget, Engineering, and other departments are represented as offices in document routing. They are not separate application roles. The deployed role values are `staff`, `head`, and `admin`. There is no separate COA Auditor application role.

## Important Included Behavior

- Registering a physically held document generates a signed QR label and automatically records its initial IN movement.
- A scan may use the camera, an uploaded QR image, or a manually entered tracking ID.
- IN and OUT movements validate the signed QR, scan session, custody state, destination, and duplicate conditions.
- OUT requires a destination, and only the selected destination office may record the next IN.
- Returning a folder is an OUT movement to the previous holder with a required reason.
- Document requests are a separate workflow from physical QR custody. Requests can be for access or pull-out and can progress through the statuses allowed by the implementation.
- COA flow reports are generated by the authorized admin; COA Auditor is therefore not shown as a system actor.
